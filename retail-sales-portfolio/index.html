Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence solution engineered with Microsoft Power BI, Microsoft Excel, Power Query (M), and DAX—transforming raw omnichannel sales records into an interactive Star Schema decision-support system.

01 / Overview

Project Overview & Business Objectives

Designed to help retail and commercial leadership move beyond static spreadsheets by unifying sales transactions, product costs, regional targets, and customer segments into a single interactive analytics layer.

01

Centralized Revenue & Margin Visibility

Consolidate multi-period sales orders, COGS, and discount records from Microsoft Excel into an automated Power BI reporting model for executive monitoring.

02

Discount & Profitability Governance

Evaluate how promotional discounting across product categories impacts net profitability and identify margin compression thresholds.

03

Regional & Channel Benchmarking

Compare regional revenue against assigned targets, rank market performance, and track omnichannel contribution across online, mobile, retail, and B2B direct sales.

04

Self-Service Executive Filtering

Empower stakeholders with dynamic slicers, Time Intelligence comparisons (YTD, YoY, MoM), and drill-down product tables without manual spreadsheet rework.

04 / ETL Pipeline

Data Preparation & Transformation Process

A structured Excel-to-Power BI preparation workflow built in Power Query (M) to guarantee clean data types, referential integrity, and fast VertiPaq compression.

STEP 01

Excel Source Ingestion

Connected Power BI Desktop to the multi-sheet Excel sales workbook containing transactional order rows and master lookup tables (Products, Customers, Regions, Calendar).

STEP 02

Power Query (M) Cleaning

Promoted headers, enforced explicit column data types (Date, Currency, Percentage, Whole Number), trimmed text fields, and filtered null or duplicate order keys.

STEP 03

Feature Engineering

Created analytical helper columns including Discount_Tier brackets, Shipping_Days lead times, and fiscal calendar attributes (Quarter, Year_Month, Fiscal_Year).

STEP 04

Star Schema Modeling

Configured 1-to-Many (1:*) single-direction relationships from Dim_Calendar, Dim_Products, Dim_Customers, and Dim_Regions into central Fact_Sales.

06 / Stack

Technologies Used

Core Business Intelligence and data modeling tools powering the end-to-end analytics workflow.

PBI

Microsoft Power BI

Interactive multi-page report design, Star Schema relationship modeling, custom JSON theming, cross-filtering slicers, and executive KPI cards.

XLS

Microsoft Excel

Structured multi-table source workbook (Fact & Dimension sheets) plus an automated formula-driven QA reconciliation sheet to validate Power BI totals.

PQ

Power Query (M)

Repeatable ETL scripts for schema validation, data type enforcement, null handling, and conditional feature engineering prior to model load.

DAX

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

Dynamic calculation engine for profit margins, Average Order Value, Time Intelligence (TOTALYTD, SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR), and RANKX market rankings.
